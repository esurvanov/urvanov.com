If the model made facts up, give it the needed data in the new request and allow it to say “I don’t know”<a class="proof" href="https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/test-and-evaluate/strengthen-guardrails/reduce-hallucinations" target="_blank" rel="noopener" data-quote="Anthropic: “Explicitly give Claude permission to admit uncertainty. The new answer is checked the same way, and the loop repeats until it fits.
