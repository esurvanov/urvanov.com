Claude Code is not available in your country when the country is missing from Anthropic's supported-countries list, effective 12 November 2026: any country or region not listed is unsupported. The policy does not mention VPNs; it does forbid bypassing protective measures. The article covers the country table, errors, bans and appeals, travelers and telemetry settings. Date checked: 18.11.2026.

<b-chapter id="prompts" title="Ready-made prompts" icon="chat">
  <b-why>Prompts for checking settings, with no region workarounds. Paste them into a Claude Code session in a supported country.</b-why>
  <b-codebox title="1. Which data settings are set" copy>
    Show which of the environment variables DISABLE_TELEMETRY, DISABLE_ERROR_REPORTING, DISABLE_FEEDBACK_COMMAND, CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_FEEDBACK_SURVEY and CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_NONESSENTIAL_TRAFFIC are set right now. Do not change any files; print a table: variable, value, what it turns off.
  </b-codebox>
  <b-codebox title="2. A short access memo" copy>
    I will paste the text of anthropic.com/supported-countries. Write a five-point memo for my team: who the restriction applies to, the three criteria (person's location, incorporation, ownership), what "not listed" means. Keep quotes in English and add nothing of your own.
  </b-codebox>
  <b-codebox title="3. Which addresses the network team must allow" copy>
    Open code.claude.com/docs/en/network-config and find the section "Network access requirements". Print a table: address, what it is needed for, optional or required. Quote the page's wording in English and add nothing of your own.
  </b-codebox>
  <b-why>A prompt is the text you give the agent. The first prompt asks the agent to read environment variables, named settings that the terminal passes to the program, and to print them in a table, which takes under a minute. If a variable is empty, the flow it controls is on: metrics, error reports and /feedback are on by default for a direct connection. The second prompt works on text you paste yourself and tells the agent to keep quotes in English, so the memo stays tied to the policy wording. The third prompt asks the agent to read the network page and list the addresses a firewall must allow; the table it returns is the same list the network team needs for a 403. None of the three touches a region, an account or a payment, and all three can run in a session started from a country on the list.</b-why>

  <b-faq-cards>
    <q t="Why is Claude Code not available in my country?">Because the country is not on Anthropic's supported-countries list, and any country or region not listed is unsupported. The policy took effect on 12.11.2026 and covers Claude Code, Claude.ai, the API and models via cloud platforms and resellers. The rule looks at where a person sits, where the company is registered and who owns it, so a plan or a key does not change the answer. The installer message for this case is “App unavailable in region”. Source: anthropic.com/supported-countries. Date checked: 18.11.2026.</q>
    <q t="Which countries support Claude Code?">The list has 185 entries on 18.11.2026, including Spain, Mexico, France, Canada, Thailand, Vietnam, Georgia, Kazakhstan, India, Nigeria and the Philippines. China, Hong Kong, Iran, Belarus, Cuba, Venezuela and Russia are not on it, and Ukraine is listed with named exceptions. Territories of a listed country are also supported. Date checked: 18.11.2026. The list changes, so open the current page and read its effective date before relying on this.</q>
    <q t="Will a VPN get me banned?">The region policy and the help article on bans do not mention VPNs; a VPN is a service that makes a connection look as if it comes from another place. Account creation from an unsupported location is a named ban reason, and the terms forbid bypassing protective measures. A conclusion about VPNs is therefore an interpretation, and the pages do not say how Anthropic determines location. Access can be suspended without notice, so the risk sits with the account.</q>
    <q t="What do I do if my account is banned?">Log in to claude.ai with the banned account and fill out the appeal form (claude.ai/restricted). After a usage-policy ban, signing in remains possible to export or delete data, and the help center says reply times are currently longer than normal without giving a figure. A hold on an organization has its own button, “Request a review”, and a warning you dispute goes to the email address linked in the article. The <a href="https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Usage Policy</a> forbids a new account to get around a ban.</q>
